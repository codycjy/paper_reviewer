However, the slower convergence of the zeroth-order optimizer remains a challenge, which recent research addresses by incorporating Hessian information to accelerate training, although storing even the diagonal Hessian requires memory equivalent to that of the model weights, leading to significant memory usage. To mitigate this problem, we propose a novel approach that integrates the block coordinate descent (BCD) method with a Hessian-informed zeroth-order optimizer, allowing us to treat model layers as separate blocks and update only a subset of layers per training iteration, thereby reducing memory requirements and accelerating convergence.
