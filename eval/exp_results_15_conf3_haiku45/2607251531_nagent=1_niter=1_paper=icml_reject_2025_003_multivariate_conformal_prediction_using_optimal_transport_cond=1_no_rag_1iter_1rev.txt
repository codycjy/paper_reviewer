      "summary_comment": "This paper presents a theoretically sound and novel approach to multivariate conformal prediction by leveraging optimal transport theory. The key innovation—using the optimal Brenier map to define a scalar ranking for multivariate scores—is elegant and principled. The coverage guarantee under arbitrary approximations of the transport map (Proposition 3.4) is a notable theoretical contribution. However, the paper is held back by weak experimental validation: the results presentation is visually unclear, lacks statistical rigor (no confidence intervals or significance tests), and the experimental design does not adequately explore the curse of dimensionality that the authors themselves acknowledge as a fundamental limitation. The hyperparameter choices (ε and m) require careful tuning with unclear guidance for practitioners. While the method shows promise and the theory is solid, the empirical evidence for practical superiority over baselines is not convincing. The paper would benefit from clearer experimental presentation, numerical tables with error bars, and more honest discussion of when the method is (and is not) preferable to simpler alternatives like Merge-CP, particularly in high dimensions where the approach may be impractical."
